## GAME DESCRIPTION

**Request Message** is a 2D psychological horror mystery game where Danilo Trinidad, a 25-year-old man, investigates the disappearance of his childhood friend Mateo Lopez.

The game features **choice-based storytelling**, where every decision influences Danilo’s emotions and the outcome of the story. As he uncovers dark memories, haunting messages, and buried truths, players determine whether he finds peace and closure—or remains trapped in unending guilt.

## THE STORY

On a stormy night in Manila, Danilo Trinidad is haunted by the memory of his childhood friend Mateo Lopez, who mysteriously disappeared fifteen years ago. After receiving strange messages from an unknown number claiming to be Mateo, Danilo returns to his hometown to uncover the truth.

As he retraces their childhood places, he finds old clues, a diary, and disturbing visions that blur the line between guilt and reality. The deeper he goes, the more he questions whether he’s chasing the truth—or losing himself to his own delusions.

Play as Danilo and uncover the truth behind Mateo’s disappearance, where **every choice shapes his fate and sanity**.

## CONTENT WARNINGS

This game contains:
- Psychological horror
- Eerie soundscapes
- Themes of grief, trauma, regret, and unresolved emotional conflict
- Depictions of mental health struggles
- Sudden visual or audio effects that may cause discomfort

This game is intended for **players aged 18 and above**. Viewer and player discretion is strongly advised.

If you feel overwhelmed or are struggling with your mental health, please pause the game and seek support. Your well-being is more important than finishing it.

## CONTROLS

**Default bindings**  
*(All keys can be customized in Settings > Controls)*

- **Move Up:** W
- **Move Down:** S
- **Move Left:** A  
- **Move Right:** D  
- **Run:** Shift  
- **Next Dialogue:** Z  
- **Map:** M  
- **Flashlight:** F  
- **Pause / Menu:** Esc  

You can freely rebind any control to your preference in-game.
